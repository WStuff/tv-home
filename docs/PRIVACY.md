# TV Home privacy statement

Effective date: 2026-10-21

TV Home is an offline Android TV launcher.

## Data collection

TV Home does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. It contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, account, or tracking SDKs and does not request Android's `INTERNET` permission.

## Data stored on the television

The following choices are stored locally in the app's private storage:

- Selected language.
- Dock and Home-app selections.
- App order and launcher preferences.
- Chosen background.
- First-run setup completion state.

Uninstalling TV Home removes these local choices.

## Installed apps and network state

As a launcher, TV Home reads the list of launchable applications installed on the television so it can display Home and **All apps**. It reads Wi-Fi, Ethernet, and VPN connection state only to display the network-status pill. This information remains on the device.

## Third-party apps

Opening an app transfers control to that app. Its own privacy policy and network behaviour then apply independently of TV Home.

## Contact

Use the repository's private security-reporting channel for privacy or security concerns. Do not include unrelated personal information in reports.
