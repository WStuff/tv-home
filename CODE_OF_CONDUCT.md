# Code of conduct

TV Home is built by people with different levels of Android experience, different devices, and different first languages. Help keep the project welcoming, practical, and safe.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give specific, reproducible feedback: include the TV model, Android version, TV Home version, and remote steps when relevant.
- Remove IP addresses, Wi-Fi names, account details, device identifiers, and ADB keys from screenshots and logs.
- Respect maintainers' decisions about product scope, especially TV Home's offline, ad-free, remote-first design.
- Credit other people's work and follow the repository's license.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, threats, discriminatory language, sexualised content, or personal attacks.
- Publishing another person's private information.
- Spam, affiliate links, impersonation, or presenting someone else's work as your own.
- Instructions intended to damage devices or compromise accounts.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when these rules are broken. Report security-sensitive matters privately using the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). For other conduct reports, contact a repository maintainer privately through their GitHub profile.

This plain-language policy is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
